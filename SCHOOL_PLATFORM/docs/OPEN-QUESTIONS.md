# Decisions required from the school

1. Country/province, public or independent status, and the regulator/reporting exports required?
2. Learner count now and in five years; staff, guardians, concurrent users, courses and peak submission size?
3. Existing SIS/accounting/timetable/identity systems and data quality? Which remains authoritative?
4. Grades, subjects, terms, timetable cycle, attendance rules, promotion and weighted-mark policies?
5. Fee structure, exemptions, discounts, sponsors, payment provider, accounting package and reconciliation rules?
6. Who may see legal names, contact details, marks, fee status, health/accessibility information and safeguarding cases?
7. Authentication options for learners without phones; guardian identity proofing and shared-device policy?
8. Home-language and accessibility needs; low-bandwidth/offline expectations?
9. Hosting country, budget, support hours, recovery objectives, procurement/security constraints?
10. Data retention and archival requirements; existing consent/privacy notices and Information Officer decisions?
11. Which Canvas functionality is mandatory for launch: Classic/New Quizzes, Studio, conferencing, plagiarism, mobile apps?
12. School name, domain, logo, colour/typography system, and desired product name?
